Our Services

Business Incorporation & Registration

They assist entrepreneurs in choosing and legalizing their business structures:

  • Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) Incorporation
  • Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Registration
  • One Person Company (OPC) Registration
  • Public Limited Company Registration
  • Sole Proprietorship & Partnership Firm Registration
  • Section 8 Company (Non-Profit/NGO) Registration
  • Subsidiary of a Foreign Company (Foreign Direct Investment / FDI setups)

Intellectual Property (IPR) Services

They help businesses secure and protect their brand assets:

  • Trademark Services: Trademark search, registration, objection replies, hearing attendance, and trademark renewal.
  • Copyright Registration: For software, literature, artistic works, and music.
  • Patent Filing: Provisional and complete patent specifications.

Tax & Regulatory Registrations

To legally operate in India, they help procure necessary tax numbers and government licenses:

  • GST Registration (Goods and Services Tax) & GST Filing
  • IEC Code (Import Export Code) for international trade
  • MSME / Udyam Registration (for small business government benefits)
  • FSSAI License (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) for food businesses
  • Professional Tax (PT) & Shop and Establishment Act Registration
  • PF & ESI Registration (Provident Fund & Employee State Insurance for payroll compliance)
  • Startup India Recognition (DPIIT registration for tax exemptions)

Corporate Compliance & Maintenance

They act as corporate secretarial partners to manage annual legal mandates:

  • Annual ROC Compliances: Filing of financial statements (AOC-4) and annual returns (MGT-7) with the Registrar of Companies.
  • LLP Annual Compliances (Form 8 and Form 11).
  • Director Services: Director Identification Number (DIN) activation, KYC updates, and adding/removing directors.
  • Share Capital Management: Increasing authorized capital, issuing share certificates, and transferring shares.
  • Winding Up / Company Closure: Legal closure of defunct businesses via the Fast Track Exit route.

Accounting, Bookkeeping & CFO Services

  • Day-to-day accounting and financial bookkeeping.
  • Income Tax Return (ITR) filing for companies and individuals.
  • TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) calculation and quarterly return filing.
  • Virtual CFO services for strategic financial advice.
